About

I am a Research Associate with the FOCAS Lab at IISc Bengaluru, where I work with Dr. Pushpak Jagtap on safe control and robotic autonomy. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from IIT Delhi in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Shubhendu Bhasin, as a recipient of the Prime Minister’s Research Fellowship (PMRF). Before that, I completed my B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering at NIT Durgapur (2016–2020).

My research focuses on safe and feasible adaptive control of uncertain dynamical systems, with particular emphasis on satisfying state and actuator constraints and establishing verifiable feasibility guarantees. I am interested in translating these methods to robotics and autonomous systems, where theoretical guarantees must remain valid under uncertainty and real-world limitations.

Uncertain Nonlinear Systems · Lyapunov and Barrier Methods · Constrained Adaptive Control · Safety-Critical Control · Robotics

Research

Classical model reference adaptive control (MRAC) guarantees tracking under uncertainty, but does not account for user-defined bounds on states or inputs. Optimization-based alternatives such as MPC and control barrier functions need real-time optimization and good model knowledge. My work develops adaptive, optimization-free controllers that enforce state, input and input-rate constraints under bounded disturbances, together with verifiable feasibility conditions that tell in advance whether a safe control policy exists.

State and input constrained MRAC for uncertain LTI systems

A saturated adaptive controller combined with a barrier Lyapunov function keeps the plant state and control input within prescribed bounds, with robustness to unmatched disturbances and sufficient feasibility conditions.

CDC 2022 · IEEE TAC 2026

Uncertain Euler–Lagrange systems and robotics

Extension to nonlinear Euler–Lagrange dynamics (robot manipulators, mechanical systems) with constraint-compliant adaptive tracking, validated on manipulator and 2-DoF helicopter models.

ACC 2023 · European Journal of Control 2026 · ICC 2025 Workshop

Input amplitude and rate limits

A two-layer barrier Lyapunov design that constrains plant states, input amplitude and input rate simultaneously for uncertain MIMO systems.

International Journal of Systems Science 2025

Time-varying state and input constraints

Adaptive control with constraints that change over time, with feasibility conditions that can be checked offline.
